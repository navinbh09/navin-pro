Research Experience

Undergraduate Researcher, SURGE Program
May 2026 – Present · Washington University in St. Louis · Advisor: Dr. Xianglin Li

Research Project: Flow Channel Optimization for Heat and Mass Transfer

  • Conducting computational fluid dynamics research on flow-channel optimization for enhanced heat and mass transfer.
  • Developing OpenFOAM workflows spanning geometry creation, mesh generation, solver configuration, convergence monitoring, and post-processing using ParaView.
  • Implementing topology optimization using the Method of Moving Asymptotes (MMA) to generate optimized flow-channel architectures.
  • Evaluating designs using pressure drop, temperature distribution, heat-transfer performance, and thermal uniformity.
  • Validating computational models against published benchmark studies.
Research Assistant, Department of Physics
Feb 2024 – Aug 2025 · Truman State University · Advisor: Dr. Rasanjali Jayathissa

Research Project: Dye-Sensitized Solar Cells Using Natural-Dye Sensitizers

  • Investigated plant-derived natural dyes as alternative sensitizers for dye-sensitized solar cells (DSSCs).
  • Designed experiments for natural-dye extraction and characterization using UV-Visible spectroscopy and Fourier Transform Infrared Spectroscopy (FTIR).
  • Fabricated and tested 30+ laboratory-scale DSSCs and characterized performance using Electrochemical Impedance Spectroscopy (EIS) and current-voltage measurements from a solar simulator (max PCE ≈ 9.90%, 55% FF).
  • Analyzed experimental data to evaluate relationships among dye properties, material characteristics, and photovoltaic performance.
  • Presented findings at the 2025 APS Prairie Section Meeting.
